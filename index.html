ABOUT OUR SCHOOL

A Place Where Learning Comes Alive

Jnyanabindu Public School, also known as JPS Dambal, is committed to providing quality education in a supportive and encouraging environment.

We encourage students to develop academic skills, confidence, creativity, discipline and good values. Our goal is to help every child discover their potential.
